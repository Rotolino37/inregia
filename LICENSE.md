### LibreOffice

InRegia does not include LibreOffice. At installation, or later at the user's request,
the installer (or the app) downloads the official LibreOffice package from
The Document Foundation and extracts it into the `engine` folder. LibreOffice is licensed under the
[Mozilla Public License 2.0](https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/) and is used as a
separate program to convert presentations.
